Smart beats only frugal

Frugal savers cut back. Smart savers also earn cash back on spending they’d do anyway, and real interest on money they’re already saving.

1Learn 2Try it 3Check

1Learn

  1. Cash backMoney back on spending you’d do anyway. On $10,000 a year, a 3% card returns $300.
  2. Interest incomeA high-yield savings account can pay 4–5% while many big banks pay 0.01%. On $10,000 that’s $400–500 a year instead of about $1 — for moving the money once.

Quick check

When does cash back actually help you?

Interest at 20%+ wipes out 1–3% cash back. Pay in full or the “reward” becomes a trap.
