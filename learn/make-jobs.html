Your pay goes up when your skills go up

Working more hours has a hard ceiling. Building a skill raises what you earn per hour — that’s how income grows without burning out.

1Learn 2Try it 3Check

1Learn

  1. Hours have a ceilingThere are only so many hours in a week — and school takes most of them. If your only lever is “work more,” your income maxes out fast.
  2. Skills don’tTutoring, video editing, design, coding help, photography — every skill you add raises your hourly rate. Skills compound like money.

ExampleMaya tutors math at $15/hr freshman year. By senior year she adds video editing and charges $35/hr. Same hours, more than double the money — because she leveled up her skills.

Remember

Why do skilled side hustles (coding, design, tutoring) generally pay more than simple tasks?
