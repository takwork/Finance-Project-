Side hustles that fit around school

You don’t need a business plan or a loan. Most teen hustles start with a skill you already have — or can learn free online — and a few people who trust you.

1Learn 2Try it 3Check

1Learn

Good fits for school are flexible, local or remote, and don’t need big loans or inventory. Start small, prove you can deliver, then raise your rate as your portfolio grows.

2Try it Activity

Pick a hustle to see how to start

Most need zero startup money and work around school hours.

Tutoring$15–30/hr

Help classmates or younger kids with subjects you crush. Start with friends, then ask teachers to spread the word. Zero cost to start.

Free to startWorks around school
Video editing$20–50 per project

Local businesses, coaches, and creators need Reels/TikToks edited. Learn CapCut free. Charge per project and build a portfolio.

Builds a skillWork from home
Graphic design$25–60 per project

Canva is free. Make logos, flyers, and posts for local shops or school clubs. Show 3 samples to land your first client.

Canva is freePortfolio piece
Babysitting$12–20/hr

Still one of the most reliable teen jobs. Tell neighbors and family friends. CPR certification lets you charge more.

Always in demandCert = higher pay
Social media$15–40 per project

Most small businesses have no real Instagram strategy. Offer to run theirs monthly. Make 3 sample posts first.

Coding / web help$20–50/hr

Learn HTML/CSS free at freeCodeCamp.org. Even basic website fixes are valuable to local businesses.
