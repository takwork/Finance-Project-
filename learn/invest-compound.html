The magic of compounding

Compound interest pays you on your money and on the interest you’ve already earned. Over decades, that’s the gap between “okay” and life-changing.

Quick math: the Rule of 72

Divide 72 by your yearly return to estimate how many years your money takes to double. At ~7% a year, 72 ÷ 7 ≈ 10 years.

It works both ways: compounding builds wealth for investors — and buries people who only pay credit card minimums. At 21% APR, debt doubles in about 3.4 years.
